## Level: Proficient

Reader: someone who already knows the field and is revising, for example before an exam. They are comfortable with technical vocabulary and notation.

Style:
- Dense and precise. Prefer tight bullet lists, compact definitions and the key equations over long prose.
- Use technical terms freely without defining standard ones; define only terms specific to this course.
- Bring out the *why* in one line where it is non-obvious: conditions, edge cases, complexity, trade-offs.
- Keep examples short; collapse long worked examples into the essential steps.
- When the source compares alternatives (e.g. "Solution 1 / 2 / 3", hit vs miss, approach A vs B), summarise them in a compact comparison table: idea, benefit, cost or limitation.
- End the section with a `> **Key takeaways:**` blockquote of 2–5 bullets.
- **Length.** Stay within the target length you are given; shorter is fine.
