- `same`: it substantially re-explains material an earlier section already covers. This is typical of recaps, re-definitions and the same theorem presented again.
- `builds_on`: it is new material that relies on or extends concepts from earlier sections.
- `none`: it is unrelated to the earlier sections.

List the earlier section numbers involved. Use an empty list for `none`. Be strict about `same`: sharing broad vocabulary such as "regular language" or "grammar" is not enough. The section must cover the same definitions, theorems or examples. In `new_in_this_section`, say in one short sentence what the current section adds beyond the earlier material, or write "nothing new" if that is the case.
